# On Board LA

I want to create an "LA Onboarding" site for civic engagement focused on the City of Los Angeles. Everything that a new resident should need.

The landing page is simple: it has "LA Onboarding" and a bar to type in an address. Underneath that it says: "Not sure? Check out the onboarding for these famous spots." and lists landmarks like Griffith Observatory, Venice Canals, and the Watts Towers.

After the user types in their address (or clicks a landmark), they are taken to the Bento Dashboard. The top navigation allows switching between:
1. Bento Dashboard:
- Squares infill with their City Council representative (photo, contact, constituent services), their local Neighborhood Council (meeting times, website, agenda), newsletters they should sign up for (hyperlocal and citywide independent press), local history and lore, essential apps they should download (MyLA311, NotifyLA, Metro TAP, LA Public Library, etc.), and logistics (trash day, street sweeping schedule).
2. Newcomer Checklist:
- A phased 30-day onboarding checklist (Day 1, Week 1, Month 1) with action items to get civically plugged in, with persistent check-off progress.
3. Interactive Map:
- Interactive map showing council district boundaries, neighborhood council boundaries, and civic points of interest alongside the localized feed.

If a user enters an address outside City of LA jurisdiction (such as West Hollywood, Santa Monica, Beverly Hills, or Culver City), display a friendly note explaining that they are in an independent municipality and link them to their city's civic portal.
